FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Capital Trust Bank a real bank?

No. This is a demonstration site. Balances, cards and transactions are dummy data, and no real money is held or moved.

Which accounts can I open?

Savings, current, business and joint accounts. You choose the type during registration.

How do I get a card?

Open live support from your dashboard and tell us which card you would like. Our team issues it to your account and it appears on your Card page.

How long does a payment take?

A payment shows as processing until it has been checked, then changes to completed. Your dashboard updates on its own.

Which languages are supported?

You can switch the site to any of 133 languages using the selector at the top of every page. Translation needs an internet connection.

Ready to open your account?

It takes about two minutes, and you get your account number straight away.
